Product Vision

Antigravity Travel is an AI-powered travel advisor that helps users discover, plan, optimize, and understand journeys.

The system optimizes for meaningful experiences rather than maximum attraction count.

Core Philosophy

Antigravity plans journeys, not attraction lists.

Antigravity plans memories, not logistics.

Antigravity acts as a travel advisor rather than a travel booking engine.
